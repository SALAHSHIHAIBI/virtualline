HoneyLine.

A complete packing line, live. Honey jars come in at one end and leave as a wrapped pallet. Every machine moves, every box is tracked, and the line screen updates counts, speeds and status in real time.

The line, start to finish.

  1. Case erector
  2. Partition inserter
  3. Case packer
  4. Pad inserter
  5. Case sealer
  6. Checkweigher and labeller
  7. Palletiser
  8. Stretch wrapper

Take a closer look.

  • Case erector. Flat blanks become open cases.

  • Partition inserter. Dividers drop in so jars can’t touch.

  • Case packer. Jars are picked and placed from above.

  • Case sealer. Flaps fold and tape seals the top.

  • Checkweigher. Every case is weighed, accepted or rejected.

  • Palletiser. The robot builds the pallet, tier by tier.

  • Stretch wrapper. The finished pallet is wrapped and ready.

A line screen that actually runs.

The HMI shows live counts, speeds and the status of every machine. Stop one and watch the line react.

Made for packaging machinery builders, food and drink producers, and robot and palletising integrators.
