PW-600.

A rotary parts washer, simulated down to the water. Dirty baskets in, clean parts out.

The wash cycle, in seven steps.

  1. Load
  2. Flood
  3. Ultrasonic
  4. Spray
  5. Drain and filter
  6. Rinse and dry
  7. Unload
