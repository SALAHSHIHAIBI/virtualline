Loading shuttle

Dirty baskets ride in on the shuttle and lift into the chamber. You can drive it from the HMI or step through it frame by frame.

The wash cycle.

01/ 07

  1. Load

    The shuttle lifts a basket of dirty parts into the chamber.

  2. Flood

    Wash water fills the chamber around the basket.

  3. Ultrasonic

    The drum turns while ultrasound lifts the grime.

  4. Spray

    High-pressure jets hit every face of every part.

  5. Drain and filter

    Dirty water drains away and runs through the filters.

  6. Rinse and dry

    A clean rinse, then hot air until the parts are dry.

  7. Unload

    Clean parts roll out to the unload station.
