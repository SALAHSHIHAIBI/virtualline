From drawings
to a running twin.

Four steps. Send us what you have, and we’ll have it ready before your show.

  1. Brief

    Tell us the machine, the show date and what the twin is for.

  2. Build

    We model the machine from your CAD or photos.

  3. Review

    You walk through it and ask for changes.

  4. Deliver

    You get the VR build, the film or the training app, ready for the show.

What we need from you.

The earlier we get CAD and the show date, the more realistic the result.

CAD files.

STEP, IGES or native, if you have them.

Photos and video.

The machine running, from every side.

Logo and colours.

For nameplates, signs and the HMI.

Your show date.

So we can plan backwards from it.
