How it works

From drawings to a running twin in four steps.

Send us what you have. We build, you review, and it's ready before your show.

  1. 1

    Brief

    Your machine, your show date and what the twin is for.

  2. 2

    Build

    We model the machine from your CAD or photos.

  3. 3

    Review

    You check it and ask for changes.

  4. 4

    Deliver

    VR build, film and / or training app, ready for the show.

What we need from you

  • CAD files STEP, IGES or native, if you have them
  • Photos & video of the machine running, from all sides
  • Logo & colours for nameplates, signs and the HMI
  • Show date so we can plan backwards

The earlier we get CAD and the show date, the more realistic the result.
