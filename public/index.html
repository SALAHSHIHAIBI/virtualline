The problem

Your best machine can't always be in the room.

It can't come to the show

Too big, too heavy and too costly to ship, install and run on a stand.

Visitors walk past the stand

Brochures and posters don't stop anyone. A moving machine does.

Buyers can't picture the line

A new line is hard to sell before it's built. 2D drawings don't show it working.

Training on live machines is slow

New operators learn on real equipment, which costs downtime and carries risk.

FAQ

Questions we often hear.

We don't have CAD. Can you still build it?

Yes. Photos, drawings and measurements are enough to start. We model the rest.

Our machine is confidential.

We can show the outside and the process only, and keep the inside detail private.

We only need it for one show.

The same model becomes your film, website content and training tool, so it keeps working long after the show.

Isn't this expensive?

Compare it with shipping, installing and running the real machine at every show, every year. You pay for the model once.

What equipment do we need at the stand?

A VR headset, a laptop or stand screen, and optionally a game controller. No shipping, no install, no power or water for the machine.
