Walk the HoneyLine.

01/ 08

  1. Case erector

    Flat blanks fold into open cases and drop onto the line.

  2. Partition inserter

    Dividers drop in so the jars never touch.

  3. Case packer

    Jars are picked from the conveyor and placed from above.

  4. Pad inserter

    A top pad goes in over the jars before the case closes.

  5. Case sealer

    Flaps fold down and tape seals the top.

  6. Checkweigher

    Every case is weighed, then accepted or rejected.

  7. Palletiser

    The robot picks, places and adds a tier sheet, layer by layer.

  8. Stretch wrapper

    The finished pallet is wrapped and ready to ship.
